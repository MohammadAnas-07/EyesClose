When someone is gone, make everything easier for the people they leave behind.

After a death in the family, every bank, phone company, pension office and payment app has its own way of doing things. Answer about ten questions and you get a list of what to close first, who to contact, and a link to each official page.

Start the checklist

Free, and there is no sign-up.

Your answers stay on your phone. We do not collect them.

Nothing you type is sent to us or saved. Close the page and the answers are gone. Letters are made on your phone as well.

How it works

  1. 1

    Answer about ten short questions. Which banks they used, their mobile network, any pension or insurance. If you do not know, choose "Not sure".

  2. 2

    Get your checklist. Steps are grouped into the first 7 days, weeks 2 to 4, and later. Each one says who to contact.

  3. 3

    Print a letter where it helps. For a bank or Vi, fill in two names and a date, then print it. Write account and phone numbers by hand, sign it, and take it with their own form.

What you get today

  • A checklist built from 33 possible steps. You only see the ones that match your answers.
  • 23 of those steps link to the official page they come from, such as the RBI, EPFO, a bank or a phone company. For the other 10 we have not found an official page yet, and the step says so.
  • Two letters you can print: a covering letter for a bank, and a request letter for Vi.
  • Short notes for harder cases, such as no death certificate yet, a locked phone, or a joint account.

Who this is for

Anyone in India who is sorting out a family member's accounts after they have died. That is often a son, daughter, wife or husband doing it for the first time. The app is in English, in short sentences.

What it does not do

  • This is general information, not legal advice.
  • Each step links to the official page it comes from, or says that we are still looking for one.
  • No lawyer or chartered accountant has checked our content yet. We are working on it, and each step is marked "Not yet checked by a professional" until then.
  • A letter goes with the bank's or company's own form. It does not replace it.
  • We do not contact anyone or file anything for you.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The checklist and both letters are free, and there is no account to make.

Do you keep my answers?

No. Your answers stay in the browser on your phone and are not sent to us. When you close or refresh the page, they are gone. On a computer, anyone can check this in the browser's developer tools, which list every request the page makes.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is general information based on official pages, and each step links to its source. If there is a will, a nominee or a family disagreement, a lawyer can tell you what applies to you.

What if I don't have the death certificate yet?

You can still start. The checklist adds a step for it: a death should be reported to the local registrar within 21 days, and there is no fee within that time. Many other steps need the certificate, so it helps to get it early.

Which places does it cover?

SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Punjab National Bank, Bank of Baroda, Kotak Mahindra Bank and Canara Bank by name, and other banks in general. Jio, Airtel, Vi and BSNL. PhonePe, Google Pay and Paytm. EPFO and central government pension. Life, health and vehicle insurance, loans and credit cards. Google, Facebook, Netflix and Amazon accounts. Registering the death itself.

Ready when you are

You can stop at any time. It is fine to do one step today and the rest later.
