How we handle your data

Draft. A professional will review this page before launch.

Short version: what you type into EyesClose stays on your phone. We do not receive it and we do not keep it.

Your answers

The questions and the checklist run inside your browser. Your answers are kept in the page's memory while it is open. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are not saved on your phone in cookies or browser storage. When you close or refresh the page, they are gone.

Letters

A letter is made on your phone from the names and date you type. The PDF file goes straight to your phone's downloads. We never see it. Account numbers, phone numbers and addresses are not typed into the app at all; you write them on the printed letter.

What we do not use

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising or tracking tools, and no scripts or fonts from other companies. The pages load files only from our own site.

The home page has one small script of our own, menu.js. It closes the phone menu after you tap a link in it. It does not read anything you type and does not send anything. The help pages and this page have no script.

Links to official pages

Each checklist step links to an official page, such as a bank or the RBI. When you open one, you are on their site and their privacy rules apply. Our pages ask your browser not to tell them which page you came from.

Our hosting provider

The site is hosted by Vercel. When your browser asks for a page, Vercel receives the request like any web host does. Its privacy notice says it collects information about traffic to the sites it hosts, such as the visitor's IP address, the city and country worked out from it, and details of the browser and device.

Vercel's documentation says request logs are kept for a limited time that depends on the plan, for example one hour on the free plan. These requests never include your answers, because your answers are not sent anywhere.

If this changes

If we ever add something that collects data, such as a way to ask for updates by email, it will ask first, collect as little as it can, and be described on this page before it goes live.
